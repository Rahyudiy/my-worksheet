2 • A STRUCTURED FLOW

Record once, every report follows.

Every transaction moves through the same flow each period. You record it once, and every report above it is built automatically.

1

Sales, expenses, or invoices recorded via app or AI Employee chat.

Accounts update running balances without manual re-keying.

3 • THE BASICS

The numbers match because they are checked as they are entered.

Bookkeeping becomes exhausting when the mismatch only shows up at month end. My Worksheet moves the check to the moment you record.

01

Debits and credits are checked on every entry

A mismatch shows up when the transaction is recorded, not at closing. The trial balance at period end is simply read.

02

One record, every report

The journal, the ledger, and every financial report come from the same transactions. Correct one transaction and the reports follow.

03

Room to grow with the business

One account can hold several companies, many branches, and several currencies, with the interface in eight languages.
